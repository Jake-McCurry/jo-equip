description: Why the CC0 Spurgeon markdown can't be trusted as-is, and how "verified originals only" is decided.
---
User chose "verified originals only" (Nov 2026): publish a sermon only when its wording matches a scan of an original printing almost word for word.

**Why:** lyteword/chspurgeon-sermons (CC0) descends from the Ages Software edition, which modernized many sermons (capitalized He/His for God, ye→you, reworded KJV quotes). CCEL uses the same edition and restricts republication.

**How to apply:**
- Compare case-sensitively. Per-1,000-word case differences are bimodal: ≤4 means faithful and ≥9 means modernized. Archaic-word replacements are a second signal.
- Use only institutional Internet Archive scans. Uploader-made "Spurgeon…Pt" PDFs are the modern edition and would verify the modernized text against itself.
- Catalog titles and dates on IA can be wrong; judge a scan by its OCR content.
- Verified sermons still contain some Americanized spellings (honor/labour mixed). Disclose this; don't "fix" it.
- OCR preaching dates can be misread. Keep a date only when its weekday matches the printed service.
- Full verification takes ~10 min with Pool(6); Pool(8) ran out of memory.
