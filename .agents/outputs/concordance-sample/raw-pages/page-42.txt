1 Peter 2:18-20 Servants, be subject to your masters with all fear;
not only to the good and gentle, but also to the froward. 19 For this
is thankworthy, if a man for conscience toward God endure grief, suf-
fering wrongfully. 20 For what glory is it, if, when ye be buffeted for
your faults, ye shall take it patiently? but if, when ye do well, and suf-
fer for it, ye take it patiently, this is acceptable with God.
