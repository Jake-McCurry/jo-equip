Goal

We need reliable GA4 reporting for:

each individual PDF
the number of unique users who initiated a download of that PDF
total download events as a secondary metric

Google Analytics Enhanced Measurement already generates the native file_download event. We also created a custom pdf_download event for this Replit-built SPA.

In manual Tag Assistant testing, one normal PDF click currently produces:

pdf_download × 1
file_download × 1

However, historical GA4 data shows that pdf_download sometimes records materially fewer users/events than file_download, while some PDFs match almost exactly. I want to determine whether the custom implementation is missing legitimate PDF clicks.
