What I would avoid
Don’t add generic praying-hands, open-Bible, or sunset photos simply to decorate pages.
Don’t generate fake portraits of Ashley or Joni and present them as the actual people. Use a properly licensed photograph of Joni if desired; Ashley’s story works better as a designed testimony box.
Don’t put important wording inside generated images. Generate the artwork without words and add clean, editable labels afterward.
Don’t use small floating images. Full-width visuals or clearly defined half-page panels will perform better on phones.
Keep response areas spacious. The writing experience is one of this version’s strengths.

If prioritizing only five additions, I would choose pages 5, 12, 18, 24–26, and 35, plus replace the existing diagrams on pages 16–17.
