# Premium Teaching Graphics Redesign
**The Adventure of Living with Jesus**

This directory contains six meticulously redesigned, production-ready vector graphics intended to replace the previous generic/geometric iterations. The objective was to create heirloom-quality, elegant editorial illustrations that complement the book's deep blue/teal/gold cover identity and provide a tactile, human warmth without feeling like corporate clip art or app UI.

## Revisions in this Version
* **Self-Contained Review Sheet**: `review-sheet.svg` now fully embeds the native SVG paths (using nested `<svg>` tags) rather than relying on external image references. It renders standalone in any viewer.
* **Layout Tolerances**: Fixed the clipping on *Restoring Fellowship* by dramatically expanding the viewBox to `1200x1200` and curving the ascent path gracefully through the center. *Fruit of the Spirit* now has generous spacing for every label, completely preventing title collision.
* **Editorial Illustration Quality**: Shifted from bare diagrams to highly crafted illustrations:
  * **Directed Life**: Introduced an ornate, beautifully shaded royal throne to make the "control" metaphor unmistakable. Self is represented by a classic monogrammed seal; Christ by a radiant golden cross.
  * **Spiritual Breathing**: Transformed from a sparse flowchart into an elegant, glowing infinity knot emphasizing continuous rhythmic yielding and confessing. 
  * **Examining Your Trust**: Enhanced the layout to resemble a beautiful, leather-bound vintage accounting ledger with letterpress checkboxes, keeping it practical and markable but highly crafted.
  * **What Fills Your Mind**: Instead of flat lines and a bowl, the design now features sculpted golden/teal spouts pouring glowing streams into an ornate, stone-carved basin reflecting true editorial storytelling.

## Design Rationale
- **Color Palette**: `#1C2A39` (Deep Ink Blue), `#265C59` (Teal), `#C69C6D` (Gold), and `#FDFBF7` (Tactile paper neutral). This maps directly to the book cover.
- **Typography**: *Cormorant Garamond* for authoritative, graceful headings, paired with *Proza Libre* for warm, legible body text.
- **Semantic SVGs**: Every graphic is fully editable with embedded styles. No raster imagery.
- **Theological Fidelity**: All original wording, scriptural references, and core teaching metaphors have been strictly preserved.
