“Let not the wise man boast in his wisdom…
But let the one who boasts boast in this: that he understands and knows me.”
— Jeremiah 9:24

“What comes into our minds when we think about God is the most important thing about us.” — A.W. Tozer

Dr. Bill Bright wrote:

“In the quiet sanctuary of the human heart, few pursuits are more vital than the earnest contemplation of who God truly is. The more we know about God, the more we will love Him, trust Him, and obey Him. Understanding His character is the foundation for a victorious Christian life.”

This book is an invitation to that pursuit.

Across the following chapters we will explore twenty key attributes of God, grouped into four foundational categories. These are not distant theological concepts. They are living realities that shape how we pray, how we endure trials, how we love others, and how we face each new day.

J. I. Packer wrote, “Once you become aware that the main business that you are here for is to know God, most of life’s problems fall into place of their own accord.” Charles Spurgeon urged, “Plunge yourself in the Godhead’s deepest sea… I know nothing which can so comfort the soul… as a devout musing upon the subject of the Godhead.”

That is the supreme pursuit of the heart: not merely to learn facts about God, but to understand and know Him. Knowledge that remains only in the mind can puff us up. Knowledge that moves to the heart produces worship, trust, obedience, and peace.

It is our prayer that as you journey through these pages, the Holy Spirit will open your eyes to behold the Lord in fresh and transformative ways. For our God is worthy—infinitely worthy—of our highest thoughts, our deepest love, and our most wholehearted devotion.
